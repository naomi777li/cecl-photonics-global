# CECL Search Snippet Experiments

## 2026-11-01 — VCSEL product page intent alignment

- **GSC property:** `sc-domain:ceclphotonics.com`
- **Window:** 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-30 (last 3 months selected; latest complete day shown by GSC)
- **Page:** `/products/vcsel-chips`
- **Baseline:** 55 impressions, 0 clicks, 0% CTR, average position 57.7.
- **Observed queries:** “multi junction vcsel chips market” (23 impressions), “asia pacific multi-junction vcsel chips market” (14), “us multi-junction vcsel chips market” (13), and “vcsel chip bonding” (1).
- **Interpretation:** The leading queries seek market sizing rather than procurement, and the page is far outside the first two result pages. Snippet CTR alone is not the main constraint. The page already has model-family and technical content; the limited metadata change makes its supplier and sensing intent explicit without claiming a ranking gain.
- **Change:** Give the English and Chinese product URLs a clear supplier/selection title and describe the page's documented 850 nm/940 nm families, application scope, published output/drive/beam data, and catalog/engineering-review route.
- **Research input:** [ams OSRAM's VCSEL portfolio](https://ams-osram.com/products/lasers/color-lasers-vcsel) and [Lumentum's sensing VCSEL page](https://www.lumentum.com/en/products/sensing-vcsel) organize product discovery around device family, application, wavelength and linked technical resources. These pages informed the information hierarchy only; their specifications or claims were not transferred to CECL.
- **Follow-up:** Recheck this page's query mix, impressions, clicks and average position after 28 days. Do not interpret movement as a causal CTR improvement unless query composition and position are considered.
