# CECL Photonics multilingual roadmap

Updated: 2026-09-30

## Recommendation

Keep English and Simplified Chinese as the complete, maintained languages now. Prepare the route and content model for additional locales, but do not publish thin machine-translated copies.

Suggested expansion order:

1. Spanish — first full additional language when Latin American search impressions or distributor inquiries justify it.
2. Portuguese — Brazil-focused product, OEM/ODM and contact pages after Spanish or when Brazil demand appears first.
3. German — high-value technical and medical-device market; use native technical review.
4. Arabic — start with selected medical-beauty/OEM landing pages for the UAE and Saudi Arabia rather than translating the entire site.
5. French — add after German if France, Francophone Europe or North Africa becomes a measurable lead source.

Japanese and Korean should wait for a local partner, native technical reviewer and confirmed documentation workflow. Russian is not a current priority.

## Decision rules

- Review Google Search Console by country, query language, impressions and non-brand searches every month.
- Review GA4 by country, landing page, engaged sessions and inquiry events.
- Open a new language only when a market shows sustained qualified interest or a distributor/project requires it.
- Translate the full main content, metadata, structured data, calls to action and legal/contact text for every published locale.
- Give every locale its own URL and reciprocal `hreflang`; use English as `x-default` until a neutral selector is needed.
- Do not auto-redirect visitors by IP or browser language. Keep a visible language switcher.
- Require native review for specifications, medical/beauty claims, compliance language and commercial terms.
